02 / THE STORY

FROM COMPUTER
TO CONSOLE.

THE BEGINNING

IT STARTED
WITH PASSION.

For Siddhartha Katiyar, music was never just a hobby. It was a passion that slowly transformed into a profession after years of learning, experimenting and believing in himself.

Born in Kanpur, Siddhartha grew up around computers, video games and music. His curiosity about technology eventually became the foundation for his musical journey.

THE JOURNEY
01

THE COMPUTER YEARS

As a child, Siddhartha was fascinated by computers, software and video games. At the same time, music was becoming an equally important part of his life.

02

EXPERIMENTING WITH MUSIC

He began exploring different genres, creating remixes and learning how technology could transform music. For years, he experimented without knowing where the journey would eventually lead.

03

THE VIDEO THAT CHANGED EVERYTHING

One day, Siddhartha watched a DJ performing and mixing remixes. He realised that the music he was creating on his computer could become a live experience.

04

LEARNING TO DJ

He started teaching himself DJing, understanding mixing techniques, experimenting with sounds and developing his own approach to music.

05

WHEN NOBODY BELIEVED

The beginning was not easy. Not everyone believed DJing could become a successful career. But Siddhartha continued learning, practising and improving.

06

PASSION BECAME A PROFESSION

What once looked like an unrealistic dream slowly became a real career — built on curiosity, discipline, persistence and a love for music.

THE PHILOSOPHY

BELIEVE IN
YOURSELF.

If you have a dream, work for it. If you have a passion, develop it. And if nobody believes in you at the beginning, believe in yourself enough to keep going.
